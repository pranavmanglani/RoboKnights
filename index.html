01 / WHO WE ARE

NOT JUST BUILDING ROBOTS.
BUILDING WHAT'S NEXT.

RoboKnights is a student-led robotics team driven by curiosity, creativity and the desire to solve real engineering challenges. We bring together people who love to design, build, test and improve.

From the first sketch to the final test, every project is an opportunity to learn something new, work as a team and push an idea further than we thought possible.
